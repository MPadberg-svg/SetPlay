## ⚽ KickOff Arena — MENA Broadcast Hub

Most IPTV apps force you to scroll through thousands of unorganized channels right before kickoff. **Set ▶ Play** puts live football front and center:

- **🔄 Real-Time Live Score Polling (`SportAPI.ai` + `TheSportsDB`)**
  Background coroutine sync automatically updates live scores, halftime transitions (`HT`), and match minutes (`1'`–`90+'`) in **Morocco / MENA (`GMT+1`)** time without manual refreshing.
- **📌 Sticky Frosted Matchday Scrubber & League Dock**
  Switch effortlessly across a **5-Day Broadcast Timeline** (`Yesterday`, `Today`, `Tomorrow`, `+2 Days`, `+3 Days`) and horizontal tournament capsules (`UEFA Champions League`, `La Liga`, `Premier League`, `Serie A`, `Botola Pro`, `CAF Champions League`, `Roshn Saudi League`) that stay pinned in a frosted obsidian-ocean glass header while scrolling.
- **🏟️ Featured Clash Spotlight Banner**
  Showcases the #1 marquee match of the night with **Dual-Club Ambient Stadium Lighting**, 44dp backlit club crests, a **5-Match Club Form Guide (`W • D • L`)**, and a recessed digital stadium scoreboard well.
- **📡 Verified MENA Zone Broadcaster Engine**
  Automatically resolves the exact MENA channel carrying each game:
  - **beIN Sports MENA**: Displays exact channel & tier (`beIN Sports 1`, `beIN Sports 2 4K`, `beIN Sports Max 1`, `beIN Sports Xtra 1`, `beIN Sports AFC`).
  - **Regional Rights Holders**: Displays `AD Sports Premium 1/2` (Serie A), `SSC 1 HD / SSC Extra` (Saudi Pro League), and `Arryadia TNT HD` (Botola Pro).
- **⚡ Dual-Stage Broadcast Launch Deck (`[ 📺 Channel + 4K/HD | (▶) WATCH LIVE ]`)**
  Pairs a frosted broadcaster pod and `4K`/`HD` quality tag with an illuminated floodlight `WATCH` pill, dark circular Play Medallion, and an **animated 3-bar broadcast equalizer** during live games.
- **📋 Match Center Dossier 2.0**
  Tap any match card to open a 5-tab interactive dossier:
  1. **Summary** — 1st Half & 2nd Half chronological goals, cards, substitutions, referee & stadium info.
  2. **Lineups** — Interactive **2D Tactical Pitch** with formations, player ratings, coaches, bench & injuries.
  3. **Stats** — Possession, Expected Goals (`xG`), shots on target, big chances & pass accuracy bars.
  4. **H2H & Table** — Head-to-head history and live competition standings.
  5. **TV Guide** — Official MENA channels, commentators, satellite frequencies & 1-tap launch.

## 🏗️ Architecture & Engineering

- **100% Kotlin & Jetpack Compose (Material 3)**: Unidirectional data flow (`StateFlow` + `collectAsStateWithLifecycle`), spring-physics micro-interactions, and zero-box typographic hierarchy.
- **High-Scale Room Database (`~289,000+` Streams)**: Bounded batch transactions, background coroutine dispatchers, and local full-text search indexing for instant filtering across 56K+ Live channels, 182K+ VOD movies, and 49K+ Series.
- **Media3 / ExoPlayer (`1.5.1`)**: Hardware-accelerated HLS (`M3U8`) and MPEG-TS stream playback with automatic format fallback, custom track selection, and lifecycle-aware playback.
